Search and Rescue Drone Nagivation

Drone Camera View

TJHSST Computer Systems Senior Research Lab for Audrey Dedicatoria

Autonomous drones have implementations in many fields of work such as delivery, defense, and Search-and-Rescue. In the context of Search-and-Rescue, drones are extremely useful for scouting for survivors and patrolling unsafe/harder to reach areas in the event of a natural disaster.

While many modern day Search-and-Rescue drones are used for outdoor survivor detection, there is a limited amount of drones that can navigate through collapsed or potentially indoor areas like caves when searching for survivors.

By implementing autonomous Search-and-Rescue qualities on a smaller, nimbler vehicle, autonomous drones can now access and navigate through these dangerous areas like underground caves or collapsed houses. Also, by refining a YOLO model to better detect humans from far away, it will decrease the chances of human survivors accidentally being missed by the drone’s detection system.
